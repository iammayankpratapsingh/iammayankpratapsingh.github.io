Heart rate, HRV, respiration, movement and stress readings have to travel from a BLE device to the phone and on to the cloud, intact, every night.

My contribution

Implementation

A mobile app that discovers and syncs with the device over BLE, then pushes readings to a cloud backend that keeps long-term history.
